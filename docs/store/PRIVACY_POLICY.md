# Polyglot Translate — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 29, 2026

## Data Collection

**Polyglot Translate does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

We do not track your browsing history, the content of web pages you visit, or any personally identifiable information.

## How Translation Works

When you translate a web page or selected text, the content is sent to third-party translation service providers:

- **Google Translate**
- **Microsoft Translator (Bing)**
- **Yandex Translate**
- **DeepL**

These services may process your data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control or have access to data processed by these services.

## Permissions Used

The extension requests the following permissions:

- **`<all_urls>` / `host_permissions`**: Required to inject translation scripts into web pages you choose to translate.
- **`storage`**: Stores your language preferences and settings locally on your device.
- **`activeTab`**: Allows the extension to interact with the current tab for translation.
- **`contextMenus`**: Adds "Translate page" options to the right-click context menu.
- **`webRequest`**: Reads Content-Type headers to detect PDF documents.

All of the above is processed entirely on your device or sent directly to the translation service providers you choose. We do not operate any intermediary servers.

## Text-to-Speech

When you use the "Listen" feature, audio is provided by Google or Microsoft text-to-speech services.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will update the extension and notify users through the release notes.
